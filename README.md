I've been using this self made plugin for at least a year now, so I decided to make it open sourced.

The idea of it started as simple, you time stamp your clip-able moments at stream time, export them as .srt file format, then edit the time stamps later, eradicating the need for scraping VODs, hence the name "srt manager".

but quickly it turned into an automation tool when I realized how many of repeatable tasks of editing I can automate that doesn't need creativity.

ripple delete? we already have the time stamps and the time distance between them, so why not just fine tune time stamps and automate the ripple delete between them (cutting function).

you want to add stroke to each subtitle, why not just a button adds to all of them at the same time? (add_stroke function)

you want each time stamp become a separate horizontal sequence with your outro? you already have the time stamps and ripple deleted them under the same name, so why not just have a button that makes separate sequence for each and adds them to that sequence with your outro?(splitsequences and groupsequencer functions)

you want to shake part of a video track? why not just cut that part of track and have a button shake it with your chosen pace(shaker function)

you want to turn a horizontal video to vertical? the formula is always the same, so why not just having a button for doing that and while your at it, apply your own vertical outro and header(mobalize function)

so yeah, this plugin is there to help me batch edit clips of a stream and even normal videos, really fast.
